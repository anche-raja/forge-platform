Bedrock Guardrails is its own managed policy engine. Detection runs on AWS-managed internal classifiers — entity/PII detectors, the content-filter classifiers, the PROMPT_ATTACK classifier, and literal word matching. You don't pick or pin a model for it, you don't get to change it, and it's billed per text unit evaluated rather than per model token. That's exactly why CLAUDE.md stresses it's called "as a standalone ApplyGuardrail API call — not inline with model invocation": there is no model invocation attached to it.
